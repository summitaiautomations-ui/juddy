# Disc Diving Video — Production Guide

Format lane: **zero talking, ASMR-first, satisfying freeze-on-the-pull.** Everything
below serves that. The voice of the channel is the *sound* and the *reveal*, not narration.

---

## 1. The signature "pull" moment (your hook)

This is the money shot. Make it a repeatable ritual viewers wait for.

- **Freeze + sound stack.** On the freeze frame, layer 2–3 sounds, not one:
  1. a wet *suction / plop* as the disc breaks the surface
  2. a short *water-drip trickle*
  3. a clean *bell / chime / "ding"* synced exactly to the freeze
  The chime is what makes brains itch — it's the payoff cue.
- **Pitch-ladder for rarity.** Low chime = beat-up common disc. Higher / brighter chime
  = rare or premium plastic. Viewers learn the audio language and feel the payoff before
  they read anything.
- **Micro slow-mo into the freeze.** Ramp to ~0.25x for the last 6 inches out of the
  water, *then* freeze. The deceleration sells the satisfaction.
- **Water-bead detail.** Hold the freeze long enough to see droplets on the stamp. A
  subtle zoom-in push during the freeze adds polish.

## 2. Audio (the whole point)

- **Hydrophone / contact mic** for real underwater sound — muffled gurgles, bubbles, the
  scrape of fingers through silt. Rare in this niche; instantly differentiating.
- **Binaural mic** on the surface for rinse / sort segments. Headphone viewers live for it.
- **Consistent "clean and reveal" SFX** — dunking the disc to rinse silt, the squeak of a
  thumb wiping the stamp.

## 3. Structure that keeps them watching

- **Silent count-up overlay.** Small corner tally — "Discs: 14" — ticking up per pull.
  Pure progress dopamine, no narration.
- **End-of-video sort montage.** Lay them all out, ASMR stacking / clacking of plastic,
  sorted by color or brand. Strong closer.
- **"Rare find" treatment.** Extra freeze time, a unique sound, slow rotate to show the
  stamp. Let production signal value instead of your voice.

## 4. Visual identity

- **Consistent grade** — teal-and-murk underwater, crisp pop when the disc surfaces. The
  contrast itself is satisfying.
- **GoPro chest / wrist mount** for first-person "hands finding it in the muck" POV.
- **Cold open** on the day's best pull in the first 2 seconds, then reset to the start.

## 5. Shorts strategy

Each great pull is a standalone Short: murky water → hand reaches in → slow-mo pull →
freeze + chime → cut. ~15 seconds, infinitely repeatable, funnels to the long-form.

---

## Single-video edit template

1. **0:00–0:02** Cold open — best pull of the day, full freeze + chime.
2. **0:02** Reset. Establishing underwater shot, count starts at 0.
3. **Body** Repeat the pull ritual per disc. Vary chime pitch by rarity. Keep dead air
   minimal but let big pulls *breathe*.
4. **Rare find** Whenever it happens — longer freeze, unique sound, slow stamp rotate.
5. **Closer** Sort montage + final disc count card. End on the cleanest clack.

## Finding the sound effects

### Free, no-attribution libraries (best starting point)
- **Pixabay Audio** (pixabay.com/sound-effects) — free for commercial use, no attribution
  required. Search: `bubble pop`, `water drip`, `magic chime`, `bell ding`, `coin`,
  `sparkle`. Big and safe for YouTube.
- **Freesound.org** — huge, community-uploaded. Filter by license: CC0 needs no credit;
  CC-BY needs attribution in your description. Search `hydrophone`, `underwater`,
  `water bubble`, `wet plop`. Great for the real underwater textures.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (in YouTube Studio → Audio Library) — built in, cleared for
  monetization, filter to "Sound effects." Solid for chimes, dings, whooshes.
- **Mixkit** (mixkit.co/free-sound-effects) — free, no attribution, decent UI-ding and
  notification sounds that work well as the rarity chime.

### Paid / subscription (if the channel grows)
- **Epidemic Sound** or **Artlist** — subscription covers music *and* SFX, fully cleared,
  no per-clip copyright claims. Worth it once you're posting regularly.
- **Soundsnap** / **Storyblocks** — large SFX catalogs on subscription.

### The specific sounds to grab
| Moment | Search terms |
| --- | --- |
| Surface break | `wet plop`, `water splash small`, `suction pop` |
| Drips after pull | `water drip`, `single drip`, `dripping` |
| Common disc chime | `UI ding`, `soft bell`, `notification` |
| Rare disc chime | `magic sparkle`, `bright chime`, `level up`, `coin collect` |
| Underwater bed | `hydrophone`, `underwater ambience`, `bubbles` |
| Sort montage | `plastic clack`, `poker chip`, `stacking` |

### Build your own (best ASMR payoff)
The most satisfying sounds are usually ones you record yourself:
- **Foley the chime** with a real glass, singing bowl, or bike bell — tap and pitch-shift
  in your editor for the rarity ladder.
- **Record real water** — dunk and pull a disc in a sink or tub with a phone or
  contact mic. Authentic suction beats any library plop.
- **Hydrophone** — a cheap one (~$50–100) records genuine underwater sound you literally
  cannot buy. This becomes a signature of the channel.

### Licensing reminders
- CC0 / Pixabay / Mixkit / YouTube Audio Library = safe, no credit needed.
- CC-BY = usable but **list the source + author in your video description**.
- Avoid random SFX ripped from other videos — that's how you get copyright claims.
- Keep a `sounds/CREDITS.txt` noting where each file came from so attribution is never a
  scramble later.
